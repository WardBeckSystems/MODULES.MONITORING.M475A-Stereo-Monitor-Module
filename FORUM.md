## MONO to STEREO conversion & STEREO to MONO conversion

_WBS TECHNICAL SUPPORT · Feb 14, 2014|47_

> Hi folks...
>
> I searched this forum for a thread discussing MONO to STEREO conversion of a 2042, and if there is one, I did not find it. We aren't the first to tackle this issue (WBS did apparently have a stereo conversion kit after all) but I doubt we'll be the last either. 
>
> Special thanks to Anthony who's already supplied a wealth of info via this site and answered my "Stereo Conversion 101" questions. I'll post further questions and the info thus far absorbed here to help add to the knowledge base. (What a brilliant & valuable resource this site is!) 
>
> We have DUAL goals in mind with the STEREO / MONO biz-ness:
>
> 1: To convert the 2042 Monitoring section to stereo - avoiding the rather annoying issue of having two independant Volume knobs for both Left and Right (M1 and M2) speakers. 
>
> 2: To equip the console with a TRUE MONO output to check compatibility of the stereo mix in mono.
>
> Our 2042 console uses the M475 monitor modules. These are mono modules, and do not seem hard to come across. (We have several spares if anyone's interested). WBS also had an M475A Stereo Module. These, I am told, are difficult to find. (pls contact me if you have one)
>
> The 475A Stereo Module has a MONO button. This lets the engineer hear how the stereo mix translates in mono. I'm told is there is a 6db increase in volume upon hitting the mono button on this module. (watch your speakers!)
>
> The 475 is a MONO module. I have found that creative mis-use of the selector buttons (a la 1176) allows me to route both M1 and M2 to the same speaker via this method. Clever I thought. However.. I've been informed by wiser blokes than myself... that this is not TRUE MONO. (shame that) 
>
> There is another WBS module which gets mentioned when discussing /searching these topics too. The M495 Stereo Monitoring Module. The M495 looks like a good option, but according to posts elsewhere on this site has some quirks (I'm coming to that) and it does not appear to have a MONO button either.
>
> WBS gurus - please correct me if I'm spreading misinformation!
>
> The "quirks" of the M495 Module relate to how it functions in SOLO mode: "it doesnt truely solo, it actually just adds the solo to the master signal... the M475's actually mute when the solo is pushed -if you wire up the controle voltage right") (The M495 is however said to be a good option for running headphones.) 
>
> "Another option is to use a pair of 475's as a stereo module with a dual 5k pot." This option is interesting. Short of finding an M475A module... it seems ideal.
>
> Exactly how is this done? Could somebody... you know... explain it to me like I was a bass guitarist?
>
> The L2042 manual speaks of the SOLO bus being wired to the MON 1. When using M1 & M2 as left and right, the SOLO function works a treat in the left speaker (MON 1) which mutes the other source material. Meanwhile M2 continues as if the SOLO function were irrelevant to it's function. Which it is. The SOLO bus isn't wired to MON 2 according to the manual.
>
> If using a "stereo pair" of M475's with a dual 5K pot my assumption is that SOLO function will still only affect MON 1 - is this correct?
>
> Is wiring the SOLO bus so it feeds both MON 1 & MON 2 (L&R) modules a simple mod? 
>
> I suspect & hope so. But it'd be nice to get clarity from a guru. I'm sure others bass guitarists will benefit too - when they start buying cool olde consoles.
>
> (btw - before anyone goes for my throat re: how SOLO buttons ruined mixing, or re: how it's irrelevant because you just hit SOLO in Pro Tools.... I sorta agree with all of you. But there's something... kinda Star Trek cool about sitting at the 2042 and having the SOLO/MUTE functions working. It... you know - hints at the ubercoolTM future light years behind us. And you can make things sound sooo good out of context... haha. The 2042 is a thing of some beauty - and as it's gonna be our main driver, fed by our 1204 sidecar, our wish is to keep some of the console's original design as as a respectful nod)
>
> Ah yes.... the true MONO output. A summing amp is required for this purpose yes? 
>
> Could an M477 be used to sum the M1 & M2 busses and allow us to get a true picture of how our mix would sound in MONO? How would this be wired /monitored? 
>
> Long post, and many questions... thanks to any & all who can help.
>
> Cheers,
>
